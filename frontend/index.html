Straight talk

What is real, and what is an estimate.

What we do

Rank destinations for your trip type, month, budget and length with a model trained for exactly that. Build a day-by-day route from the places in our database. Estimate the total from typical hotel prices and daily spending.

Every thumbs-up and thumbs-down you give is stored with the trip it was about, so the ranking can be retrained on real opinions.

What we don't do

We don't book anything or check live availability. Prices are estimates from a hand-collected dataset: check them before you pay. Hotel pins on the map are approximate. Flight and hotel buttons open Google Flights and Booking.com searches in a new tab.

The model was first trained on simulated travellers, so its scores describe how well it learned our rules, not a guarantee that you'll love a place.

Questions

Good to know.

Is it free?

Yes. There is nothing to pay for. This is a student project, so please treat it as a planning aid and not a booking service.

How accurate are the prices?

They are estimates built from typical hotel rates and daily spending in each city. They are good for comparing places and setting a budget, not for quoting a final price.

Do I need an account?

You can try the ranking above without one. An account lets you chat, build itineraries, save trips and share links.

Can I switch between chat and the guided pills?

Any time. Both write to the same memory, so what you chose in one shows up in the other.

What happens to my data?

We store your account, chat memory, saved trips and feedback so the app works. You can download everything or delete your account from Settings. Read the privacy page for details.

Can I plan trips outside Europe?

Not yet. The dataset covers a set of European cities and the cities are chosen to be easy to combine into routes.
